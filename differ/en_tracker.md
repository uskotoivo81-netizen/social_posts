# SpeedyIndex AI Overview Rank Tracker: Google Positions & Citations

An AI Overview rank tracker answers a specific question: where does a site appear in Google desktop results for a set of keywords, and when Google shows an AI Overview, does it cite that site? SpeedyIndex Rank Tracker covers both parts in one project, and it checks Google desktop results only, for a country or a city you choose; mobile results and other search engines are not supported. You can set the depth to Top-10, Top-30, Top-50 or Top-100, add up to 10 competitor domains, and see AI Overview citations and quotes for every keyword where they appear.

Each report has four sections: Summary, Positions, SERP competitors, and AI Overviews. Summary shows visibility, average position, Top-3 and Top-10 counts, AI Overview citations, and change since the last check. Positions lists every keyword with its place, SERP features, ranking URL, and trend. Visibility is your share of possible clicks across all keywords in the project, based on positions and how many keywords were found. The report also shows every site that reaches the Top-10 and your place among them. A short walkthrough shows how to connect SpeedyIndex to Claude for rank tracking checks.

## Report details

The rank tracker stores history so you can compare a check with the previous one: keywords that entered or dropped out of the Top-10, visibility movement, and average position changes. You can add tags and target URLs to keywords, run checks manually or on a schedule, and export results. For AI Overviews, the report shows whether Google's AI Overview cites your site, what it says about you, and how that changes from check to check.

## Pricing and tokens

SpeedyIndex Rank Tracker has no subscription. You pay with tokens only for the checks you run. The regular price is 4 tokens per keyword for Top-10 depth; from November 2 to December 2, 2026, the launch price is 2 tokens per keyword. New accounts receive 100 free tokens on sign-up with no card required, and tokens never expire. Deeper pages beyond the Top-10 are loaded only while your site or a competitor is still not found, so you pay only for pages actually loaded. The same token balance covers rank tracking, link indexing, index checks for Google, Bing, and Yandex, and backlink checks.

## Project setup

Creating a project takes three steps. First, enter your domain and keywords, one per line with optional tags and target URLs, then choose a country or city and the depth. Second, add up to 10 competitor domains and choose a manual check or a schedule. Third, read the report. You can export results and follow the trend history up to 90 checks for position movement and AI Overview data.

## AI Overview tracking

For keywords where Google shows an AI Overview, the [SpeedyIndex rank tracker for Google positions](https://en.speedyindex.com/google-rank-tracker/) records whether the AI Overview cites your site, what it says about you, and how that changes between checks. The Positions section shows the ranking URL and SERP features for every keyword, while the AI Overviews section gives a per-keyword view of citations and quotes. This makes it easy to see which pages AI Overviews pick up and how those citations vary over time.
